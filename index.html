Executive Summary

Abstract & Research Objectives

Although Low-Power Wide-Area Networks (LPWANs) based on Long-Range (LoRa) Chirp Spread Spectrum (CSS) modulation promise multi-kilometer connectivity, real-world urban and campus deployments suffer severe blockage attenuation that pushes signals into the ultra-low SNR regime (below Semtech's nominal $-7.5\text{ dB}$ limit for SF7). Recent deep-learning receivers—such as CALoRa for preamble detection and NELoRa, GLoRiPHY, and LoRaSeek for symbol decoding—demonstrate substantial sensitivity gains in isolated stages, yet end-to-end physical-layer performance under realistic hardware impairments remains largely unexplored.

In this thesis, we model and benchmark the complete three-stage sequential LoRa gateway receiver pipeline (Preamble Detection $\to$ Synchronization $\to$ Symbol Decoding) across $-25\text{ dB}$ to $-10\text{ dB}$ under realistic $\pm 5\text{ ppm}$ Carrier Frequency Offset (CFO) and fractional Symbol Timing Offset ($\text{STO} \in [0, T_s/2]$). While replacing conventional preamble detection with CALoRa yields a $+1.0\text{ dB}$ end-to-end gain under clean AWGN, that sensitivity advantage vanishes completely once CFO and STO are introduced: at $-18\text{ dB}$, CALoRa detects 82% of packets, but conventional analytical up–down chirp FFT synchronization passes only 42% for CFO and 44% for STO, collapsing symbol decoding accuracy to 53%.

To overcome this Stage 2 bottleneck, we validate a "Denoise-then-Sync" proof-of-work using STFT spectrogram denoising prior to analytical up–down chirp estimation (handling $2.25$ SFD down-chirps via complex conjugation). At $-19\text{ dB}$, pre-synchronization denoising recovers buried FFT peaks with a +26.1 dB peak-to-median gain ($1.48\times \to 29.8\times$). At $-18\text{ dB}$, it boosts STO accuracy from 61% to 89% ($+28\%$) and CFO accuracy from 58% to 87% ($+29\%$), delivering an immediate +2.0 dB end-to-end compensated payload decoding improvement ($90\%$ threshold shifting from $-15.5\text{ dB}$ to $-17.5\text{ dB}$; accuracy rising from $63\%$ to $83\%$ at $-18\text{ dB}$). Finally, we propose a dedicated Dual-Slope Denoise-then-Sync Network trained natively on unaligned preamble up-chirps and SFD down-chirps to unlock practical ultra-low SNR LoRa reception.

Meskipun teknologi LoRa (Long-Range) menjanjikan jangkauan komunikasi multi-kilometer, implementasi di lingkungan perkotaan dan kampus mengalami redaman bangunan yang menjatuhkan Signal-to-Noise Ratio (SNR) ke zona ultra-low SNR (di bawah batas $-7.5\text{ dB}$ untuk SF7). Berbagai model AI terbaru telah meningkatkan deteksi preamble (CALoRa) dan dekoding simbol (GLoRiPHY, LoRaSeek), tetapi belum dievaluasi pada pipeline penerima tiga tahap secara utuh di bawah offset perangkat keras nyata.

Tesis ini membuktikan bahwa di bawah gangguan CFO ($\pm 5\text{ ppm}$) dan fractional STO ($\tau \in [0, T_s/2]$), keuntungan deteksi AI pada tahap 1 hilang sepenuhnya: pada SNR $-18\text{ dB}$, CALoRa mendeteksi 82% paket, namun sinkronisasi FFT konvensional hanya meloloskan 42% CFO dan 44% STO sehingga akurasi dekoding jatuh ke 53%. Dengan pendekatan Denoise-then-Sync (denoising spektrogram STFT sebelum sinkronisasi up–down chirp), puncak FFT yang tenggelam pada $-19\text{ dB}$ pulih sebesar +26.1 dB, akurasi STO/CFO meningkat +28–29% pada $-18\text{ dB}$, dan dekoding payload end-to-end meningkat sebesar +2.0 dB ($-15.5\text{ dB} \to -17.5\text{ dB}$).

LoRa(Long-Range) 기반 LPWAN은 장거리 저전력 IoT 통신에 널리 활용되지만, 도심 및 캠퍼스 환경에서는 장애물 감쇄로 인해 신호가 SF7 기준 복조 한계($-7.5\text{ dB}$) 이하인 초저(Ultra-Low) SNR 영역으로 급격히 저하됩니다. 최근 프리앰블 검출(CALoRa)과 심볼 복조(GLoRiPHY, LoRaSeek)에 딥러닝이 도입되었으나, 실제 하드웨어 오프셋(CFO, STO) 환경에서 3단계 전체 수신기 파이프라인을 통합 검증한 연구는 부족합니다.

본 연구는 $\pm 5\text{ ppm}$ CFO 및 분수 심볼 타이밍 오프셋($\text{STO} \in [0, T_s/2]$) 환경의 종단간 벤치마크를 통해, $-18\text{ dB}$에서 CALoRa가 82%의 패킷을 검출하더라도 기존 FFT 기반 2단계 동기화가 CFO 42%, STO 44%만 통과시켜 전체 성능 이득이 소멸함을 규명하였습니다. 이를 해결하기 위한 Denoise-then-Sync 사전 검증에서 STFT 스펙트로그램 디노이징을 동기화 전에 적용한 결과, $-19\text{ dB}$에서 피크 대 중앙값 비율이 +26.1 dB 향상되었고, $-18\text{ dB}$에서 STO(61%→89%) 및 CFO(58%→87%) 정확도가 +28~29%p 개선되었으며, 종단간 페이로드 복조 임계값이 +2.0 dB($-15.5\text{ dB} \to -17.5\text{ dB}$) 향상됨을 입증하였습니다.

Core Research Gap & Specific Objectives

Because LoRa receivers operate as a strict sequential cascade, deploying deep learning in Stage 1 (Preamble Detection) and Stage 3 (Symbol Decoding) yields no practical benefit if Stage 2 (Synchronization) fails under ultra-low SNR.

  • Objective 1: Adapt and integrate machine learning spectrogram restoration into LoRa synchronization to decouple CFO and STO under ultra-low SNR.
  • Objective 2: Evaluate end-to-end Symbol Decoding Accuracy (SDA) and synchronization recovery improvements against conventional analytical receivers.

Low-Power Wide-Area Networks (LPWANs) enable long-range telemetry across precision agriculture [1], [2], maritime monitoring, and smart cities [3], [4]. In 2024, Semtech [5], [6] reported that LoRa accounts for 30% of global LPWAN deployments with 6.9 million gateways and 350 million end nodes. Yet field studies [7], [8] confirm that blockage attenuation in urban and campus settings degrades SNR well below theoretical link budgets.

CSS BASEBAND & DECHIRPING Discrete-Time LoRa Modulation ($N = 2^{\text{SF}}$)
$$x_s[n] = \exp\!\left(j2\pi\left(\frac{n^2}{2N} + \left(\frac{s}{N} - \frac{1}{2}\right)n\right)\right), \qquad d[n] = y[n]\cdot x_0^*[n], \qquad \hat{s} = \arg\max_{k \in \{0,\dots,N-1\}} \left|\text{FFT}\{d[n]\}[k]\right|$$
Multiplying a received chirp $y[n]$ by the conjugate down-chirp $x_0^*[n]$ collapses the wideband sweep into a narrow spectral peak at bin $s$, enabling reception below $0\text{ dB}$ SNR until noise spikes exceed the coherent peak.

Hardware Offsets & Conventional Stage 2 Synchronization Failure

STO ($\tau$)

Symbol Timing Offset

Asynchronous arrival misaligns the receiver's FFT window ($\Delta k_{\text{STO}}$), shifting dechirped FFT bin indices and corrupting downstream payload symbols.

CFO ($\Delta f$)

Carrier Frequency Offset

Crystal mismatch ($5\text{ ppm}$ at $915\text{ MHz} = 4{,}575\text{ Hz}$) introduces continuous phase rotation; any residual error $> \pm 488.3\text{ Hz}$ (half bin at SF7) causes wrong symbol bins.

SFO ($\delta_s$)

Sampling Frequency Offset

ADC clock mismatch near $1\text{ MHz}$ produces cumulative phase drift that is negligible during short preambles but accumulates across longer payload frames.

Why Analytical Synchronization (Bernier et al. [13]) Breaks at Ultra-Low SNR

A single up-chirp cannot distinguish CFO from STO. Bernier et al. [13] decouple them by combining preamble up-chirp peak $k_{\text{up}}$ and SFD down-chirp peak $k_{\text{down}}$: CFO shifts both in the same direction ($k_{\text{up}} = k_{\text{CFO}} + k_{\text{STO}}$), whereas STO shifts them in opposite directions ($k_{\text{down}} = k_{\text{CFO}} - k_{\text{STO}}$):

$$\widehat{\Delta f} = \frac{B}{2N}\left(k_{\text{up}} + k_{\text{down}}\right) = \frac{k_{\text{up}} + k_{\text{down}}}{2T_s}, \qquad \hat{\tau} = \frac{T_s}{2N}\left(k_{\text{up}} - k_{\text{down}}\right) = \frac{k_{\text{up}} - k_{\text{down}}}{2B}$$

Because both estimators depend directly on raw dechirped FFT argmax peak picking, random AWGN spikes in the ultra-low SNR regime corrupt $k_{\text{up}}$ and $k_{\text{down}}$—especially across the short $2.25$ SFD down-chirps—causing complete Stage 2 breakdown.

Research Methodology & Proposed Synchronization Architecture

Unlike prior studies that evaluate preamble detection or symbol decoding in isolation, our Python-based modular simulation framework evaluates the complete three-stage sequential LoRa gateway receiver directly on unaligned, offset-impaired I/Q streams.

Step 1

Baseband Packet

2 pure-noise frames + 8 preamble up-chirps + 2 Sync Words + 2.25 SFD down-chirps + 10 random SF7 payload symbols.

Step 2

Channel Impairments

$1\text{ MHz}$ stream injected with Gaussian CFO ($\pm 5\text{ ppm}$), fractional STO ($\tau \in [0, T_s/2]$), and AWGN ($-25$ to $-10\text{ dB}$).

Step 3

Sequential 3-Stage

Stage 1 coarse detection $\to$ Stage 2 CFO/STO estimation & derotation $\to$ Stage 3 payload decoding.

Step 4

Monte Carlo Metrics

Logs Preamble Detection Rate, CFO ($\le 488.3\text{ Hz}$) & STO Success Rates, End-to-End SDA, PMR, and latency.

Proposed Stage 2 Dual-Slope Denoise-then-Sync Architecture

Off-the-shelf payload denoisers like LoRaSeek [17] cannot be dropped directly into Stage 2 without adaptation because of a fundamental domain mismatch: LoRaSeek was trained exclusively on aligned single payload up-chirps ($\text{CFO} = 0$, $\text{STO} = 0$), whereas Stage 2 must process unaligned fractional-STO windows containing both $8$ preamble up-chirps and $2.25$ opposite-slope SFD down-chirps. (In our proof-of-work, SFD down-chirps are mapped into the up-chirp domain via complex conjugation before denoising.) To eliminate boundary mismatch permanently, our proposed final thesis module bridges CALoRa (Stage 1) and LoRaSeek (Stage 3) with three submodules (Figure 5):

4.1. Baseline Symbol Demodulator Benchmark ($N = 1{,}000$ SF7 Symbols @ $-18\text{ dB}$)

Among conventional DSP methods, Naive 1/8 Decimation FFT (subsampling $1\text{ MHz} \to 125\text{ kHz}$ directly before dechirping) collapses to 14.3% accuracy due to wideband noise aliasing. Polyphase Downsampling reaches 99.2% in $37.7\text{ ms}$, but only because its Nyquist FIR low-pass filter strips $87.5\%$ ($+9.03\text{ dB}$) of out-of-band noise under idealized zero-CFO/STO sync (effectively comparable to SF10), making it an excluded theoretical reference rather than a fair SF7 baseline. Among AI models, STFT denoiser architectures—GLoRiPHY (95.2%, 5.23 s) and LoRaSeek (93.2%, 4.12 s)—outperform NELoRa (79.0%, 22.24 s) by $+14.2\%\text{--}16.2\%$ while running $4.2\times\text{--}5.4\times$ faster. LoRaSeek's speed and accuracy make it our primary Stage 3 decoder.

4.3. Proof-of-Work (1/2): FFT Peak Breakdown & Recovery via Spectrogram Denoising

At $+10\text{ dB}$ (Figure 14), dechirped preamble up-chirp ($k_{\text{up}}$, bin 30) and SFD down-chirp ($k_{\text{down}}$, bin 74) FFT peaks are razor-sharp. At $-19\text{ dB}$ (Figure 15), the true chirp peak at bin 42 is submerged beneath AWGN spikes ($\text{PMR} = 1.48\times$), causing argmax to falsely lock onto bin 101. Passing that identical $-19\text{ dB}$ signal through the LoRaSeek STFT denoiser and inverse STFT before dechirping (Figure 16) suppresses background noise and restores the exact ground-truth peak at bin 42 with a $29.8\times$ PMR (+26.1 dB peak-to-noise gain).

4.4. Proof-of-Work (2/2): Stage 2 Sync Recovery & +2.0 dB End-to-End Payload Gain

Evaluating Denoise-then-Sync across $-25\text{ dB}$ to $-10\text{ dB}$ confirms dramatic Stage 2 recovery: at $-18\text{ dB}$, CFO accuracy jumps from 58% to 83% (GLoRiPHY) and 87% (LoRaSeek, +29%) (Figure 17), while STO accuracy increases from 61% to 89% (+28%), shifting the 90% threshold by $2.1\text{ dB}$ (Figure 18). Compensating incoming frames with these denoised estimates boosts downstream payload decoding (Figure 19) by +2.0 dB at the 90% threshold ($-15.5\text{ dB} \to -17.5\text{ dB}$) and raises decoding accuracy at $-18\text{ dB}$ from 63% to 83% (+20%).

Dedicated Dual-Slope Sync Model Proof-of-work reuses off-the-shelf LoRaSeek (trained on aligned single up-chirps) via SFD conjugation, leaving fractional-STO boundary mismatch. Train the proposed Dual-Slope Unaligned Synchronization Network natively on unaligned preamble up-chirps and $2.25$ SFD down-chirps under fractional STO, CFO, and AWGN. Eliminate domain mismatch and boost sub-bin CFO/STO accuracy below $-18\text{ dB}$.
Inference Latency & Edge Efficiency STFT neural denoising requires $4.12\text{--}5.23\text{ s}$ per $1{,}000$ symbols, exceeding real-time embedded gateway budgets. Apply lightweight attention blocks, structured pruning, and INT8 quantization tailored for gateway deployment. Real-time, low-latency synchronization on commercial LoRaWAN gateways.
Multi-SF, SFO & Real SDR Testing Current evaluation focuses on synthetic SF7 AWGN + CFO/STO channels without cumulative SFO or multipath fading. Extend evaluation across $\text{SF}7\text{--}\text{SF}12$, Sampling Frequency Offset (SFO), multipath fading, and real over-the-air SDR traces. Verify robustness and field readiness across diverse urban/campus deployments.
